# Turkey-Production-Analysis
Built and back-tested a linear regression forecast on 40 years of USDA turkey production data; identified systematic overestimation against 2024 actuals and corrected it with a 20M-lb/year downward adjustment. Applied BLS Poultry CPI (vs. general CPI, r=0.61) for inflation-adjusted valuation, producing a validated 2025 production forecast.
A Graduate Business Analytics capstone ( 6 members) forecasting U.S. turkey production for 2024–2025 using 40 years of USDA historical data (1984–2023). The project combined data-gap research, descriptive trend analysis, regression-based extrapolation, and model validation to assess whether the industry's historical growth trend still holds given recent volatility (avian flu disruptions, inflation, supply chain shocks).
